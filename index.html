01 · About Me

A business-operations professional with a technology heart

I'm an Operations Manager at 30Days Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a software company based in Bahadurgarh, Haryana that builds ERP, HRMS, CRM and SaaS products. My path began with a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) from Vanish Arya Kanya Mahavidyalaya, Bahadurgarh, under Maharishi Dayanand University, Rohtak — a foundation that keeps me comfortable at the intersection of people, processes and technology.

Stepping into the software industry, I discovered that my real strength lies in making businesses run smoothly: coordinating teams, monitoring tasks and productivity, handling escalations, and supporting HRMS and CRM operations so that projects and people stay on track.

I value clarity, accountability and continuous learning. Whether it's a pending task list, a client escalation or a cross-team meeting, I like problems that need organisation, calm judgement and follow-through.
